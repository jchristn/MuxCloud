# Delete Your Account

**Last updated:** November 20, 2026

**Version:** 1.0

## Summary

- You can delete your Mux Cloud account at any time, on the website or in the **Mux Cloud** app from Lattice AI, LLC. Deletion takes effect right away and cannot be undone.
- Every connected machine is disconnected and removed, and every signed-in device is signed out.
- **Your conversations stay on your machines.** Mux Cloud never stored them, so deleting your account does not delete them.
- A small amount of information is kept for legal and security reasons.

## 1. Delete your account on the website

1. Sign in at app.usemux.ai.
2. Open **Profile and security**.
3. In the **Danger zone** section, choose **Delete account**.
4. Enter your password and choose **Delete my account**.

## 2. Delete your account in the Mux Cloud app (iPhone, iPad, or Android)

1. Open the Mux Cloud app and sign in.
2. Open **Account**.
3. Tap **Delete account**, enter your password, and confirm.

## 3. If you cannot sign in

Email hello@latticetech.ai from the email address on your account and ask us to delete it. We may ask you to confirm the request so that no one else can delete your account. We will complete verified requests within 30 days.

## 4. What is deleted

- Your account, profile, and legal acceptance records.
- Every machine record and its connection history. Each machine's credential stops working immediately; mux on that machine shows that it was signed out.
- Every sign-in session and every push notification token.

## 5. What is kept

- Security and audit records about your account, such as sign-ins and deletions, for 90 days, to protect against abuse and meet legal obligations.
- Request logs, which expire on their normal 30-day schedule.
- Copies in backups, which are removed within 30 days.

## 6. Your machines afterward

mux keeps working locally on every machine. To use a machine with Mux Cloud again, create a new account and sign in to Mux Cloud from that machine.
