## 2. Information we collect

### 2.1 Information you give us

- **Account information.** Your email address, your first and last name if you provide them, and a password. We store passwords only as a salted one-way hash (PBKDF2); we never store or see your password in readable form.
- **Legal acceptances.** Which version of the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy you accepted, and when.
- **Communications with us.** Emails you send to hello@latticetech.ai and anything you include.

### 2.2 Information about your machines

When you connect a machine, we store its name, host name, operating system description, installed mux version, a fingerprint that identifies the installation (a one-way hash, not your files), its machine credential in encrypted form, and when it was added, last connected, and last seen. For each connection we record the time, the IP address it came from, how it ended, and how much data passed through it. Machines also report how many runs are active and how many are waiting for a tool approval, so the dashboard and app can show it.

### 2.3 Traffic we relay but do not store

When you use a machine through the Service, requests and responses between your browser or app and that machine, including conversation content, files, and settings, pass through our servers. We process them in memory only as long as needed to deliver them. **We do not store their contents.** For relayed requests we keep only metadata: the time, method, a path template without your machine's own paths, status, duration, size, and your account and IP address.

### 2.4 Information collected automatically

- **Sessions.** For each sign-in: the device name your browser or app reports, its user agent, its IP address, and when the session was created, last used, and expires.
- **Request logs.** For requests to Mux Cloud's own pages and API (not relayed traffic), we keep the method, path, status, timing, IP address, and, for troubleshooting, truncated request and response bodies with credentials, cookies, and tokens removed. These logs are kept for 30 days.
- **Security and audit records.** Sign-ins and failed sign-ins, password changes, machine enrollments and revocations, and administrative actions.
- **Cookies.** The web dashboard uses two strictly necessary cookies to keep you signed in. We do not use advertising or analytics cookies.

## 6. Security

We use TLS for every connection, store passwords as salted hashes, store machine credentials encrypted, revoke sessions and machines on request, and limit sign-in attempts. Relayed traffic is decrypted in memory on our servers so it can be delivered; it is not end-to-end encrypted between your browser or app and your machine. No system is perfectly secure; protect your account with a strong password.
